iPhone tips

Keep the page open. The app asks iOS to keep the screen awake; turn brightness all the way down and lay the phone face-down. If the screen locks or you switch apps, iOS may pause the audio.

The ringer switch can mute web audio on some iOS versions. If you hear nothing, flip it off silent.

Volume here is relative to the phone's volume buttons. Set the phone volume so 100% here is the loudest you'd ever want.

Sliders use a decibel scale (100% = full, each 10 points is about 5 dB), so the ramp sounds even rather than jumping at the end.
